## The game (current truth, Aug 13 2026)

- **Spelly Zombie** — Steam app id 5050950, page in setup, not yet public.
- One to twelve players, online multiplayer only (solo play = hosting a
  lobby of one; there is no separate single-player mode).
- Genre in one line: prop hunt meets capture the flag, with hand-drawn
  magic. Casual party game, cute stylized 3D, Unity 6.
- Core mechanic: every spell is literally drawn by hand with a wand. Draw
  runes on any surface (walls, barrels, your own body), enclose them in a
  drawn circle (a seal), and the spell fires where you drew it. Two runes
  in one seal combine (heat + rock = meteor). Recognition reads YOUR
  handwriting; the game's one joke is that your handwriting is the problem.
- Two teams: **Wizards** defend a cauldron of ink (the flag); **Acolytes**
  disguise themselves as objects (prop hunt), sneak in, corrupt the
  cauldron by standing near it, and summon zombies as distractions.
  Zombies flee anyone holding a wand; they swarm only the wandless.
- Ink is the economy: drawing costs ink, the cauldron is the shared supply,
  fights are over the supply.
- Death is not spectating: dead players become ghosts who possess things
  (a wizard ghost possesses spells, an acolyte ghost possesses zombies).
  Positioning line, Marko's own: prop hunt dies because dead players sit
  in menus; here **dying is a mode, not a menu**.
- Price: **$5.99**, matching Meccha Chameleon exactly, regional prices to
  mirror theirs (Japan matters most).
- Languages at launch (interface + subtitles, no audio): English, Italian,
  Spanish-Spain, French, German, Arabic, Korean, Portuguese-Brazil,
  Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Turkish.
- Japanese localized name: スペリーゾンビ.
- Platforms: Windows only. No controller support at launch (mouse/keyboard
  only, drawing needs a mouse for now).
