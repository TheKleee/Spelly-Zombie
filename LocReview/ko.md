# Korean (ko) review sheet

File: `Assets/StreamingAssets/Loc/sz_loc_ko.json`. Register: 해체 (intimate,
no 요) for the lines that talk to the player, plain declarative for facts,
the noun form (기) for buttons, as Korean games label them. No honorifics.
