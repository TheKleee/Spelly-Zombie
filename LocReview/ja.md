# Japanese (ja) review sheet

File: `Assets/StreamingAssets/Loc/sz_loc_ja.json`. Register: plain form
throughout, the way Japanese games label buttons. Kid words in kana (なぞ,
おぼえる, もどる, えらぶ). Full-width parentheses and the middle dot, as
Japanese typography wants. Japanese has no capitals, so the English ALL CAPS
lines lose their emphasis on purpose.
