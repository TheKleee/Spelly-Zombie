promises the exact clip people came for, and it mirrors their line beat for
beat.

## 7. THE CALENDAR (from Valve's own rules)

Store page review takes 3 to 5 business days and Valve asks for it 7 business
days ahead. The page must then be PUBLIC for at least 14 days before the game
can release.

Working back from a **15 October** release:

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| **by 1 October** | page must be LIVE (the 14 day rule, hard) |
| **by 24 September** | submit for review (allowing a re-review if art bounces) |
| **NOW, this week** | submit anyway |

Submitting now instead of in September costs nothing and buys six extra weeks
of wishlist accumulation, which is the entire month-one plan. Every day the
page is not live is a day of wishlists that do not exist. The page can be
edited freely after approval; only major changes trigger another review.

Also: releasing before Next Fest forfeits Next Fest (it requires an
unreleased game). Registering costs nothing and does not bind us, so
register, then decide in September on wishlist velocity.
